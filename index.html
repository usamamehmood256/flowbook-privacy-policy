FlowBook – Terms of Service & Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

Introduction

FlowBook is a business management application designed to help businesses manage sales transactions, customer debts, and customer information.

Information Collected

FlowBook stores information entered by the user such as customer names, phone numbers, and sales records. All information is manually entered by the user.

Use of Information

The information is used only to provide application functionality including sales tracking and sending reminders via WhatsApp.

Third-Party Services

FlowBook may open WhatsApp to allow users to send reminders to customers. FlowBook does not control third-party services.

Data Security

Data entered into the application may be stored locally on the user's device. We take reasonable steps to protect user information.
