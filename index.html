FlowBook – Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

1. Introduction

FlowBook is a business management application designed to help businesses manage sales transactions, customer debts, and customer records.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the FlowBook application. By installing or using the application, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information Collected

FlowBook may store information that users voluntarily enter into the application, including:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address (optional)
  • Customer names and phone numbers
  • Sales transactions and debt records

All customer information is manually entered by the user. FlowBook does not automatically access or collect contacts from your device.

3. How Information Is Used

The information stored in FlowBook is used only to provide application functionality, including:

  • Managing sales and customer records
  • Tracking debts and payments
  • Sending payment reminders through WhatsApp

FlowBook does not sell, rent, or share personal or business data with third parties.

4. Data Storage

Data entered into FlowBook may be stored locally on the user's device. Future versions of the application may introduce optional cloud backup features.

Users are responsible for maintaining backups of their business data if required.

5. Third-Party Services

FlowBook may open third-party applications such as WhatsApp to allow users to send reminders to their customers.

FlowBook does not control and is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services. Users should review the privacy policies of those services.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information stored within the application. However, no electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

FlowBook is not intended for children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date. Continued use of the application indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
